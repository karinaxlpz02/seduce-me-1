Welcome To Shared Desires

Shared Desires is an online platform where people are invited to take a moment and reflect on their personal desires. This platform is meant for people to record a note on their desires and intimacies and share it to the library of notes. Each note engages with one another creating it’s own language where people are can read and therefore create a note as a response, creating a link to one another in this small online corner.
